Yes, **a customer organization can have more than one identity provider** when using **Workforce Direct**.

Based on the documentation, the migration process and Admin Center functionality explicitly support multiple identity providers:

- During the **Workforce Managed to Workforce Direct migration**, customers can **import multiple federations** from their existing setup. The import dialog allows selecting multiple identity providers to import.

- The Migration page instructions reference "**each identity provider listed**" and "**every identity provider**", indicating support for multiple providers.

- Organizations can have multiple identity providers with different domains assigned to each one, allowing different user groups within the same organization to authenticate through their respective IdPs.

This capability is particularly useful for organizations that need to support users from different domains or have merged with other entities that maintain separate identity infrastructure.

Based on the documentation, here's what I found about **global users**:

## Global Users (B2B Domain Users)

**Global users** are users who are part of a **global (B2B) domain** and represent an exception to the standard identity rules for **Workforce Direct** organizations.

### Key Characteristics:

**For Workforce Direct Organizations:**
- Normally, all users in a Workforce Direct org **must reside in the organization's own Identity Provider (IdP)**.
- **Exception:** Users who are part of a **global (B2B) domain** can access the organization without being in the customer's IdP.
- This allows business-to-business relationships where external users (like Tyler staff or partner organizations) can be granted access to customer workspaces.

### Common Use Case - Tyler Staff Access:

The most common example of global users are **Tyler employees** (`@tylertech.com`):
- Tyler staff members can be granted access to customer organizations as Org Admins or for support purposes
- They authenticate through Tyler's own identity system, not the customer's IdP
- This enables Tyler to provide implementation, deployment, and support services without requiring customers to add Tyler staff to their own identity provider

### Important Distinction:

**Authentication Logs Impact:**
- In Workforce Direct organizations, authentication logs in Ops Center **only show users associated with the organization's federation(s)**
- They **exclude** TylerTech users (global domain users) and Workforce Delegated users
- To see Tyler staff authentication activity, you would need to check Tyler's own identity logs

This global domain capability is essential for Tyler's operational model, allowing staff to support multiple customer organizations without requiring each customer to manage Tyler user accounts in their own identity systems.
