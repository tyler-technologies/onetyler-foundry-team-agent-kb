---

## Creating Organizations for Identity Workforce Clients

Organizations are Tyler Clients. An organization must be created for all clients that use Identity Workforce.

### Step 1: Check if Organization Already Exists

**As of April 1, 2026, Ops Center automatically creates customer organizations** when CRM account records become sales-enabled. 

Use the **Organization Identifier (CRM Customer Identifier)** to search for the organization in Ops Center. The organization likely already exists due to automatic creation.

**If the organization exists:**
- Auto-created orgs are **Workforce Direct** by default
- They do **NOT** have customer contact information or domains set initially
- To add a technical contact: Go to **Organization Details > Admins** and use the **"Use as technical contact"** option when adding an Org Admin
- To change the Identity Tier: Use the convert-org ticket (see Ops Center ticket documentation)

### Step 2: Create Organization (If Needed)

If the organization does not exist, there are two methods available:

1. **Use +Import Organization** (self-service for Workforce Direct or Workforce Delegated)
   - Requires appropriate Ops Center permissions
   - Available for orgs with valid CRM records
   
2. **Submit an Org Creation request** (required for Workforce Managed)
   - Required for Workforce Managed Identity Tier
   - Required for internal orgs without well-formed CRM records

---

### Considerations: County vs Sheriff's Office Organization Structure

When determining the appropriate organization level (County vs. Sheriff's Office), consider these scenarios:

#### Scenario 1: Existing County ORG
**Situation:** Civil software is listed in CRM under the Sheriff's Office, but the S.O. is a CRM Child, and the County is the CRM Parent.

**Action:** A new ORG should be created (if none exists) for the Sheriff's Office using the Sheriff's Office CRM Identifier as **Workforce Delegated**, with the County as the Super org.

#### Scenario 2: No County ORG (County Manages Sheriff's Office Users)
**Situation:** Civil software is listed in CRM under the Sheriff's Office, but the S.O. is a CRM Child, and the County is the CRM Parent. The County manages all users for the Sheriff's Office.

**Action:** 
- Create a County ORG as **Workforce Direct** (or use existing auto-created County org)
- Create a Sheriff's Office ORG as **Workforce Delegated** (Sub org), with the County as the Super org

#### Important Notes:
- **Identity Tier cannot be changed after org creation** (except for the narrow case of converting an UNINITIATED Workforce Direct org to Workforce Managed via ticket)
- **Workforce Delegated** (Sub orgs) delegate identity and user setup to their Super org
- Only the Super org can set up federations and add users
- Sub orgs can only add users that already exist in the Super

---

### Additional Scenarios

There are several other circumstances that could exist. **Please seek guidance from CorpDev or your team lead if:**
- The CRM hierarchy is complex or unclear
- Multiple departments share identity management
- You're unsure whether to use Workforce Direct or Workforce Delegated
- The auto-created org has the wrong Identity Tier

---

**Key Change Summary:** The major update is that most organizations are now automatically created as Workforce Direct when CRM records become sales-enabled, so your primary task shifts from "create the org" to "verify the org exists and configure it properly."
