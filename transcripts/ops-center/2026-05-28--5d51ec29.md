**Q:**

> What do I do if a customer gets an invalid link error with a magic link?

**A:**

```markdown
Based on the knowledge base, here's what to do if a customer gets an **invalid link error** with a magic link:

## Troubleshooting Invalid Magic Link Errors

### Common Causes

**Magic links expire after 7 days.** If the customer received the magic link more than 7 days ago, it will no longer work and they'll get an invalid link error.

### Solution: Resend the Magic Link

You can resend a new magic link to the customer through Ops Center:

1. **Navigate to the Organization** in Ops Center
2. Go to **Identity Workforce** section
3. Use **Setup new federation** to send a new magic link to the Org Admin

### Additional Troubleshooting Steps

**Check email delivery issues:**
- Confirm the customer has **allow-listed Tyler email domains**:
  - `tylerportico.com` (required for all orgs)
  - `Okta.com` (additional requirement for Workforce Managed orgs)
  
- Magic link emails come from these domains, and if they're blocked by the customer's email system, the customer may not receive them or they may be quarantined.

**Verify the Org Admin was set up correctly:**
- Ensure the Org Admin's email address is correct in Ops Center
- Check that the Org Admin user shows the correct status (not "Pending")

### Prevention

When handing off to customers, remind them that:
- Magic links expire in 7 days
- They should complete federation setup promptly after receiving the link
- If they need a new link, they should contact you to resend it through Ops Center
```
