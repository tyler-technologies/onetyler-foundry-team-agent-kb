Let me search for relevant information about this scenario.Let me search for more specific information about subject identifier mismatches and user profile issues.## Issue: Employee Unable to Login - Username Linked to Different Sub

When an employee can't log in and you're seeing that their username is associated with a different `sub` (subject identifier), this typically indicates a **user identity mismatch** between systems. Here's what's happening and how to address it:

### What the "Sub" Means
The `sub` claim is the **unique identifier for an authenticated user** in the identity token. It's the permanent, immutable ID that identifies a specific user account. The `preferred_username` (username) is a separate, more human-readable identifier.

### Common Causes

1. **User Profile Changes in the IdP**: If the user's profile was modified, deleted, or recreated in the customer's identity provider (like Entra ID), they may have received a new `sub` value while keeping the same username.

2. **Multiple Accounts**: The same username might exist across different identity sources or was recreated, resulting in a different underlying subject identifier.

3. **Migration or Sync Issues**: During migrations (like Workforce Managed to Workforce Direct), user mappings may become misaligned.

### How to Troubleshoot

**Is this Identity Workforce or Community Access?**
- **Identity Workforce**: Employee/staff logging in through customer's IdP (Entra ID, Google Workspace, etc.)
- **Community Access**: Citizens/residents logging in through Tyler-managed Okta

### For Identity Workforce Issues

If the Gateway error page displays a **Request ID**, you can view detailed logs in DataDog to see exactly what's happening during authentication. Reference: [Docusaurus-Identity.md](/api/tenant-knowledge-base/collections/TCP-KB-Identity/files/0c056ff0-f664-4262-839a-c70ffa59df85/download)

Reference: [Conf-IdentityTickets.md](/api/tenant-knowledge-base/collections/TCP-KB-Identity/files/68c5b3fa-1e2b-4933-b1e5-2972769932e3/download)

The Tyler Identity team can access logs and configurations to resolve the mismatch between the username and subject identifier.
