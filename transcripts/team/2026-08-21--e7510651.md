## What We Know

**Admin Center** is Tyler's administrative portal for managing workspace and product settings, including configuring Identity Providers (IdPs) for workforce authentication.

## How to Access Specific Admin Centers

To access Admin Center for a specific organization, you'll need:

1. **Organization Key or Workspace Key** - These unique identifiers determine which admin center instance you're accessing
2. **Proper Access Permissions** - You need to be provisioned as a user with admin rights for that organization

## Getting Organization/Workspace Information

The best source for viewing organizations and workspaces is **Ops Center** - Tyler's operational portal for managing organizations, workspaces, products, and deployments. If you have access to Ops Center, you can:

- View organizations you're associated with
- Retrieve organization keys and workspace information
- See product deployments
