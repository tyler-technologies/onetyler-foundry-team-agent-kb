**Q:**

> Are release notes emails sent in the dev and qa environments, or just production?

**A:**

```markdown
Based on the knowledge base, **release notes emails are only sent in production**, not in dev and QA environments.

The documentation shows three environments available for the Aligned Releases system:
- **TCPCI (dev)**: `https://api.tcpci.com/portal/platformservice/`
- **TCPQA (QA)**: `https://api.tcpqa.com/portal/platformservice/`
- **TCPPROD (production)**: `https://api.tylerportico.com/portal/platformservice/`

However, the release notification emails are specifically designed to notify **Org Admins** (client back-office users with full Admin Center access) about upcoming quarterly releases. These notifications are sent to the Org Admins as identified in Admin Center and include:
- A four-week advance notice before the release
- Up to four day-before notices (one for each cohort if a client has workspaces in all four cohorts)

Since these are client-facing communications about production releases, they would only be sent in the production environment where actual customer organizations exist.
```
