**Q:**

> how do i access audit logs in ops center

**A:**

```markdown
Based on the Ops Center documentation, here's how to access audit logs:

## Authentication Logs in Ops Center

Ops Center provides **authentication logs** that show login activity, and the availability and detail level depend on the organization's Identity Workforce tier:

### How to Access Authentication Logs

1. Go to **Ops Center** → **Organizations**
2. Select the organization you want to view logs for
3. Navigate to the **Organization Details** page
4. Look for the **Authentication logs** section

### What You'll See (varies by tier)

| Aspect | Workforce Managed | Workforce Direct |
|--------|-------------------|------------------|
| **Availability** | Ops Center and Admin Center | Ops Center only |
| **Retention** | 90 days (Okta policy) | 90 days (Gateway policy) |
| **Users reflected** | All users (including TylerTech) | Only users associated with federation(s); excludes TylerTech users |
| **Detail level** | Considerable (Okta logs) | Compact (Gateway logs) |
| **Delay** | Near instantaneous | 5–10 minute delay |
| **Activity types** | Comprehensive (users + services) | User activity only |

### Limitations for Licensing Actions

The authentication logs primarily track **user login activity**, not operational actions like product licensing. For information about who performed licensing actions or other operational changes, you would need to look at:

- **Ops telemetry** - The AWS QuickSight dashboard (see below)
- **Contact OneTyler** - For specific audit trail questions about operational actions

### Ops Telemetry (AWS QuickSight)

For broader telemetry about organizations, workspaces, and product licensing:
- Access via: https://sso.tylertech.com/app/UserHome → **Tyler Cloud Insights Center**
- Dashboard: **TCP Prod Stats**

If you need specific audit information about who licensed a product, you may need to contact the OneTyler team directly, as the current authentication logs focus on login events rather than operational actions.

---

**Citations:**
- [Docusaurus-OpsCenter.md](/api/tenant-knowledge-base/collections/OT-OpsCenter/files/b13d8410-34a5-4341-ac57-c7d6d4e64b6e/download)
```
