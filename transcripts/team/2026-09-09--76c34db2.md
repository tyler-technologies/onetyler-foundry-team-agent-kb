Based on the documentation, here's how you can view and edit workspace properties:

## Viewing and Editing Workspaces

**If you're a customer/administrator:**

You can view and edit **presentation-level settings** in **Admin Center** under the **Workspaces** section:

### What You Can Edit in Admin Center:
- **Workspace > Overview:** View workspace type (production/non-production) and status (enabled/disabled). You can also edit the workspace title (which controls Community App Directory banner text and navigation rail).
- **Workspace > Access control lists:** Create and manage ACLs for that workspace
- **Workspace > Apps:** View apps that require ACL-based access
- **Workspace > Links:** Override org-level link text/URLs for community-facing application footers
- **Workspace > Contacts:** Override org-level Business and Technical contact defaults
- **NonProduction Workspace > Banner:** Set a banner (text + color scheme) to distinguish non-prod from prod environments

### What You CANNOT Do in Admin Center:
- **Create new workspaces** — these must be provisioned by Tyler staff
- **Change licensing or product availability** — this is managed in Ops Center by Tyler staff

---

**If you're Tyler staff:**

You have full access through **Ops Center**:

Navigate to: **Ops Center > Organization Details > Manage Workspaces**

From here you can:
- Create new workspaces
- View all workspace configuration details
- Access workspace-specific Ops Apps and tools
- Manage licensing and product availability at the workspace level
