## Adding Yourself as an Org Admin

**Important:** The generic "Ops Center additional permissions" form does NOT cover this request. Instead, you need to follow the **Manager's Guide procedure**.

### Process

1. **Follow the Manager's Guide** procedure documented in Confluence:
   - **Tyler Cloud Platform (TCP) | Org Admin promotions (Admin Center access) - a Manager's guide**
   - Confluence location: `/wiki/spaces/TTI/pages/386629479/`

2. **No direct ticket URL** — This is a manager-driven workflow rather than a self-service ticket request.

### What is an Org Admin?

Organization Administrators (Org Admins) are users with:
- Access to their organization's **Admin Center**
- A role with **full access to all functionality** available within it
- Access to all Tyler solutions that participate in centralized administration

**Note:** Tyler staff can also be granted Org Admin permissions on a customer's org. When that happens, the Tyler staff member functions in a client Org Admin capacity.

### Next Steps

Access the Confluence Manager's Guide at the link above to complete your self-promotion to Org Admin. This will give you the necessary permissions to perform product operations within Ops Center.
