# Tyler Identity & Access Overview

Tyler Identity is the comprehensive identity and access management platform for the Tyler Cloud Platform. Here's what it covers:

## **Core Identity Models**

**Workforce Identity**
- For Tyler employees and customer staff members
- Three setup models:
  - **Workforce Direct**: Tyler hosts the identity provider directly
  - **Workforce Managed**: Tyler manages federation to your existing identity provider (like Azure AD/Entra ID)
  - **Workforce Delegated**: You maintain full control of your own identity provider

**Community Access**
- For external users (citizens, constituents, business users)
- Managed through CAPM (Community Access Profile Manager)
- Allows self-service registration and profile management

## **Key Components**

- **Gateway**: The central authentication and authorization service that sits between your users and Tyler applications
- **Federation**: Connect external identity providers using SAML or OIDC protocols
- **Credential Templates**: Define how users authenticate (password, MFA, external IdP, etc.)
- **Tokens & Claims**: OAuth 2.0 and OpenID Connect tokens that carry user identity and permissions
- **Single Sign-On (SSO)**: One login across multiple Tyler applications
- **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)**: Additional security layers for sensitive access

## **What would you like to know more about?**

- How to configure Gateway for your product?
- Setting up federation with your identity provider?
- Understanding tokens and claims?
- SSO and MFA behavior?
- Something else specific?

```markdown
Let me search for information about Identity Workforce product tiers.# Identity Workforce Product Tiers

There are **four** Identity Workforce models (also called tiers). These are set during organization creation in Ops Center and **cannot be changed afterward**. Each model determines how your organization federates with identity providers and how administrators initially access Admin Center.

## The Four Models

| Model | Who Controls Identity | Initial Admin Access | Where Federation is Set |
|-------|----------------------|---------------------|------------------------|
| **Workforce Direct** | Customer's own IdP | Magic link | On the org itself |
| **Workforce Managed** | Tyler-managed Okta tenant | Through Admin Center | On the org itself |
| **Workforce Delegated** | Delegated to Super org | Through Admin Center | **On the Super org** (not the Sub) |
| **Workforce Global** | Local user store (Private Preview) | Auto-creates admin account | On the org itself |

---

## Model Details

### **1. Workforce Direct (Federation-Only)**
- **What it is**: The customer federates directly to their own identity provider (e.g., Azure AD/Entra ID, Okta, etc.)
- **Identity control**: Customer has full control
- **Best for**: Organizations that want to maintain their own IdP and have IT staff to manage federation
- **Initial access**: Via a **magic link** sent to the Customer Technical Contact
- **Federation setup**: Customer IT admin configures OIDC or SAML federation on their organization
- **If federation breaks**: Use the [Reestablish Federation process](https://tylertech.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/TTI/pages/386625934/Tyler+Cloud+Platform+TCP+Reestablish+Federation+Demo)

### **2. Workforce Managed**
- **What it is**: Tyler hosts and manages an Okta tenant for the customer
- **Identity control**: Tyler manages the underlying IdP; customer manages users through Admin Center
- **Best for**: Organizations that don't want to maintain their own IdP or need Tyler to host identity
- **Initial access**: Through Admin Center credentials
- **Federation setup**: Tyler sets up the Okta tenant; federation is on the org itself
- **Note**: Requires a contract/purchase; cannot be converted from Direct unless the org is uninitiated (no federations, no non-Tyler users)

### **3. Workforce Delegated (Super/Sub Structure)**
- **What it is**: A multi-organization hierarchy where **Sub orgs delegate all federation to a Super org**
- **Identity control**: Centralized at the Super org level
- **Best for**: Large organizations with multiple divisions/subsidiaries that need centralized identity management
- **Initial access**: Through Admin Center credentials
- **Federation setup**: **Critical difference** — federation is configured on the **Super org only**, never on the Sub orgs
- **Important**: If you're working with a Sub org and need to federate, you must identify and configure the Super org

### **4. Workforce Global** *(Private Preview as of August 2026)*
- **What it is**: Integrates with a local user store that automatically creates the admin account
- **Status**: Currently in Private Preview — not generally available yet
- **Identity control**: Local user store
- **Initial access**: The local user store auto-creates the admin account
- **Federation setup**: On the org itself
- **Note**: This is a **distinct model** from Delegated (not just another name for it)

---

## Key Points to Remember

✅ **These models are NOT interchangeable** — the Identity Tier is set at org creation and cannot be changed (except in specific limited conversion scenarios for Workforce Direct → Managed)

✅ **Ask which model** if unclear — don't assume Direct just because it's common

✅ **Delegated is special** — always establish which org is the Super before giving federation instructions

✅ **Different bootstrap paths** — how the first admin gains access varies by model

---

For more information, see [FAQ-Identity.md](/api/tenant-knowledge-base/collections/TCP-KB-Identity/files/c1191e27-b7b6-49b6-bab8-d01be2a93cd5/download) and [_START_HERE.md](/api/tenant-knowledge-base/collections/TCP-KB-Identity/files/5deffa0f-1d7f-40ff-a30f-3d3aa327ec24/download).

**Which model are you working with, or do you have questions about a specific tier?**
```
