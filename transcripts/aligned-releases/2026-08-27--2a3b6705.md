**Q:**

> what is a cohort?

**A:**

```markdown
A **cohort** in the Aligned Releases system refers to one of the four weekly windows during which a General Availability (GA) feature is activated for different groups of workspaces within a quarterly release.

## Key Points about Cohorts:

- **Four cohorts per release** — Each quarterly GA release is divided into 4 cohorts, typically spaced one week apart
- **Gradual rollout** — Cohorts enable a staggered, phased activation of features rather than turning everything on at once for all customers
- **Workspace assignment** — Each workspace (customer environment) is assigned to a specific cohort (1, 2, 3, or 4) for each product
- **Cohorts apply to GA only** — The cohort system is used specifically for General Availability rollouts, not for Planned, Private Preview, or Public Preview stages

## How It Works:

When a feature is ready for GA and assigned to a release, it doesn't activate immediately for everyone. Instead:
1. Cohort 1 workspaces receive the feature in the first week
2. Cohort 2 workspaces receive it in the second week  
3. Cohort 3 workspaces receive it in the third week
4. Cohort 4 workspaces receive it in the fourth week

This phased approach allows product teams to monitor for issues and respond before all customers are affected.
```
