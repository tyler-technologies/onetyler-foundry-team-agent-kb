**Q:**

> how do I get access to SAC?

**A:**

```markdown
Based on the information available, here's how to get access to SAC:

## For Tyler Staff Making Access Requests

**Tyler staff must use their `@tylertech.com` email** to access SAC.

To make a support access request:

1. **You must be a member of a SAC User Group** - Your membership in these groups entitles you to access specific products installed against a workspace
2. **Navigate to the Support Access Center** - Use your Tyler email to log in
3. **Submit an access request** for the specific product and workspace you need

## Access Approval Process

The approval process depends on the customer organization's settings:

- **Full access (default)**: Requests are **auto-approved**
- **Limited access**: Requests require **manual approval** from the customer's Org Admin, who will receive a magic-link/approval email

## For Support Council Representatives

If you need to be added to a SAC group:
- Contact your product's **Support Council representative** who manages SAC group membership
- They can add users through the `tylertechnologiestx` Admin Center

## Compatibility Note

SAC is not compatible with all organization configurations. It requires checking both the **Identity Tier** and **Workspace OnPrem target** of the organization.
```
