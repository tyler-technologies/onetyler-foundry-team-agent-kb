**Q:**

> What products use SAC?

**A:**

```markdown
I'll search for information about what products use SAC (Support Access Center).

Based on the Support Access Center documentation, **any Tyler product that has integrated with SAC** can use Support Access Center. However, products must meet specific engineering requirements to adopt SAC.

## Requirements for Products to Use SAC

A product must meet **all** of the following requirements to integrate with Support Access Center:

1. **Integrated with Identity Workforce (Gateway)** - SAC is not supported over Workforce Managed + Okta

2. **Registered in the Tyler Cloud Platform** - Including any application definitions that expect to check user access

3. **Indicate in product registration that the product has adopted SAC** *(Note: This capability is not yet available but will be added in the future)*

4. **Adopt the Security API** (`tcp-login-security-api` v1) to query whether a user has access to your application on a given workspace

5. **Implement a webhook** to receive revocation notifications when access is revoked

## How It Works

Once a product is integrated with SAC:
- The product's **Support Council representative** creates SAC user groups and maps them to the product
- Tyler operational staff who are members of those groups can then request time-bound access to customer installations of that product
- Access requests are either auto-approved or require customer Org Admin approval, depending on the organization's settings
```
