**Q:**

> what is Support Access Center

**A:**

```markdown
**Support Access Center (SAC)** is Tyler's framework for **standardized, secure, time-bound, transparent, and customer-controlled access** to customer installations by Tyler operational staff.

## Key Features

- **Secure & Time-Bound**: Access is granted for specific time periods and automatically expires
- **Customer-Controlled**: Customers can approve or deny access requests from Tyler staff
- **Transparent**: Full audit history of who accessed what and when
- **Standardized**: Consistent access process across Tyler products

## Who Uses SAC

- **Tyler Staff**: Any Tyler employee with an `@tylertech.com` email can log in to request access to customer installations for support purposes
- **Customer Org Admins**: Approve or deny access requests from Tyler staff
- **Support Council Representatives**: Administer SAC groups and manage which Tyler staff can access which products
- **Product Engineering Teams**: Integrate their products with SAC to enable this access framework

## How It Works

1. Tyler staff request access to a specific product on a customer workspace
2. The customer organization (if configured for limited access) reviews and approves/denies the request
3. Once approved, Tyler staff gain time-limited access to provide support
4. All access is logged and auditable
5. Access can be extended or revoked as needed

## Access the Dashboard

- **TylerPortico (production)**: https://admin.tylerportico.com/platform/support-access-center/dashboard
- **TCPQA**: https://admin.tcpqa.com/platform/support-access-center/dashboard
- **TCPCI**: https://admin.tcpci.com/platform/support-access-center/dashboard
```

**Q:**

> how will the tech connect to the product to provide support

**A:**

```markdown
## How Tyler Tech Staff Connect to Products to Provide Support

Here's the complete workflow for how a Tyler technician connects to a customer's product to provide support:

### Step 1: Request Access Through SAC

The Tyler staff member submits an access request through the **Support Access Center Dashboard** by completing a 4-step wizard:

1. **Access details** 
   - Select the customer **Organization** and **Access through** value
   - Provide a **support ticket link** (can search active CRM cases to auto-fill)
   - Enter an **Access reason** for the customer to review

2. **Select products** 
   - Choose which product(s) they need access to (filtered by their SAC group memberships)
   - Must select at least one product

3. **Select workspaces** 
   - Choose which customer workspace(s) they need to access
   - Must select at least one workspace

4. **Review and submit**
   - Confirm all details and click **Request access**

### Step 2: Approval Process

What happens next depends on the customer's **Admin Center Tyler Access setting**:

- **Full access** (default): Request is **auto-approved** immediately and appears under **MyAccess** on the dashboard
- **Limited access**: Request goes to **Pending Approval** and the customer Org Admin receives an email with **Approve/Deny** buttons. The customer must explicitly approve before access is granted.

### Step 3: Technical Connection to the Product

Once approved, the Tyler staff member can connect to the product. The product uses the **Security API** to verify access:

**Security API Check:**
```
GET /platform/login-security/api/v1/checkaccess/{sub}/workspace/{workspaceKey}/app/{appRegistrationId}
```

The API returns:
- `supportAccess.hasAccess` - Whether the user has access (true/false)
- `supportAccess.expiresOn` - When the access expires
- `supportAccessUserGroups` - Which SAC groups the user belongs to

The product checks this API during login to determine if the Tyler staff member should be granted access to that specific workspace.

### Step 4: Time-Limited Access

- Access is **time-bound** and automatically expires on the date specified
- The tech can **extend access** if needed (subject to the same approval process)
- Access can be **revoked** at any time by either the Tyler staff member or the customer

### Step 5: Session Termination

When access expires or is revoked, the product receives a **`support-access-revoked` webhook event** that triggers immediate termination of any active sessions for that Tyler staff member.
```
