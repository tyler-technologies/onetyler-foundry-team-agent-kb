> tell me about credentials template and when it would apply to a product

**A:**

```markdown
## Credential Templates Overview

**In simple terms:** Credential Templates are a way for **single-tenant applications** to define OAuth credentials (like client IDs and secrets) upfront in a structured format. Instead of manually requesting credentials for each deployment, your template gets automatically applied when your product is deployed to a customer, provisioning the exact credentials your application needs.

**When it applies to your product:**

Credential Templates are specifically for **single-tenant applications** — products that are deployed separately for each customer. 

If your product is **multi-tenant** (deployed once for all customers), you don't use Credential Templates. Instead, you submit an Identity Client Ticket for each environment.

---

## How It Works

### Development Side:
- You define your credential needs in YAML files (stored in GitHub)
- Specify things like client types (Login with PKCE, Client Credentials Flow, etc.), scopes, redirect URIs, and more
- These templates go through your normal PR review process, which helps catch security issues like excessive permissions

### Deployment Side:
- When your product is deployed to a customer workspace, the template is automatically "applied"
- The system provisions the actual OAuth credentials your application needs at runtime
- For **Community Access**, this means Okta client credentials are automatically created
- For **Workforce** (Gateway) products, the Gateway returns the client ID and secret for your application

---

## Why Use Credential Templates?

✅ **Security**: Prevents manual errors like adding too many permissions or forgetting required ones  
✅ **Consistency**: Same credentials configuration across all deployments  
✅ **Automation**: No manual credential requests during deployment  
✅ **Version Control**: Changes are tracked in GitHub with proper review

---

- [Template Schema Details](https://docs.tylerdev.io/identity/shared/credential-templates/schemas/credentialtemplate)
- [Getting Started with Community Access](https://docs.tylerdev.io/identity/community/getting-started)
```
